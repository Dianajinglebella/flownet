        x.Sources and search
        
            
        Nothing is taken. Everything links home.
        
            
        Where the photos come from
        
          
        
          
        Flownet keeps the author, link, time, tags and permitted image features, shows authorized previews or embeds, and always links back to the original. You can follow a whole site, even if the photographer never joins.
        
        
        
        
        
          
        
            
        SourcePriorityAccessRole
        
            
              
        Photographers’ own sitesP0RSS, Atom, JSON Feed, WordPress REST APICore: the original source
        
              
        Bluesky (AT Protocol)P0searchPosts API, later the Jetstream feedReal-time discovery
        
              
        Mastodon (ActivityPub)P0Hashtag timelines, media-only postsOpen social network
        
              
        FlickrP1API; commercial use needs a licencePhotography depth
        
              
        Wikimedia CommonsP1Official API + per-image licence checkClassic archive
        
              
        Instagram, TikTokP0 · creator-connectedOfficial APIs, only when a creator links their own account: Instagram API with Instagram Login (Business or Creator accounts), TikTok Display APIWhere most photographers already post. They choose which posts appear; every one links back. No scraping.
        
                    
          
        
        
        
        
        Publicly visible is not the same as free to download, cache or redistribute.
        

        
        
          
        Three paths to the right photo
        
          
        
            
        Path 1
        Keywords
        東京 ストリート写真香港街頭攝影35mm filmavailable light
        Text can’t decide alone: a Nikon billboard in the shot isn’t an ad.
        
            
        Path 2
        Image meaning
        CLIP or SigLIP embeddings place photos and your prompt in one space, so a rainy black-and-white Tokyo street lands close to your request. It can judge a film look, not prove a photo was shot on film.
        
            
        Path 3
        Creator network
        When one photographer keeps matching your taste, suggest following their own site by RSS. They never need to join Flownet.
        
          
        
          
        A few hundred candidates per path · merge · dedupe · review · score · ranked on open, no live web crawl.

          •

        xi.Ranking
        
            
        Nothing hidden behind the glass.
        
            
        A score you can read
        
          
        
          
        Six signals, weighted by you. Move them and watch one Tokyo street photo rise or fall.
        
        
        
        
        
          
        
            
        S = 100 × (0.25T + 0.25V + 0.15G + 0.15F + 0.10I + 0.10Q)
        
            
        
              
        SignalWeightSharePhoto
        
              
        
            
        
          
        
          
        
            Worked example · Tokyo street photo, posted 2 days ago
            
        89.1 / 100
        
            
        Starting weights for the MVP, to be tuned with real feedback.
        
            
          
        
        
        
        
        Likes and views are not in the formula. A photographer with 200 followers can outrank one with 2 million.
        

        
        
          
        
            Freshness
            
        Fresh first, classics apart
        
            
        Freshness halves every few days. Left alone, it would bury everything older, so classics live in a pool of their own, scored without time decay.
        
            
        F = 2^(−d / 3.5)
        
            
            
          
        
          
        
            
            
        Curve from the formula; not measured user data.

        Tokyo · 5New York · 5Hong Kong · 4Other cities · 6

        xii.Ownership and protocol
        
            
        The work stays where it was made. Only the current moves.
        
            
        The photo and the feed are separate things
        
          
        
          
        Built on the open AT Protocol stack. No new blockchain, no lock-in. The same rules may return different results on different providers, and that is the point: your rules travel.
        
        
        
        
        
          
        Original workalice.photo/posts/tokyo-rain
        Stays on Alice’s own site, under her control
        
          
        My feed rulesmydomain.com/feeds/photography.json
        Prompt, filters, weights and timing belong to me
        
          
        Ranking providerany compatible service
        Switch providers; nobody has to republish

        xiv.Business model
        
            
        Paid for what it gives, never for what it hides.
        
            
        Every revenue line is tied to value someone can see
        
          
        
          
        Free for everyday use. Those who get more from the network pay for it. Organic rank is never for sale; sponsored posts are labeled and kept apart.
        
        
        
        
        
          
        People
        Premium search, persistent tracking and advanced personal algorithms
        
          
        Creators
        Hosting, pro tools, growth analytics and commerce features
        
          
        Curators
        Paid channels and curation services, with a revenue share
        
          
        Institutions
        Vertical discovery tools for photography, education and research
        
          
        Brands
        Clearly labeled sponsorships, kept apart from organic ranking
        
        
        
        
        Payments run on Stripe: subscriptions on the web, payouts to creators and curators through Stripe Connect, and RevenueCat for App Store and Google Play subscriptions. Flownet never sees or stores card numbers.
